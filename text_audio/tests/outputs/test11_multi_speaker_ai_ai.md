# Summary
The conversation explores the value and meaning of reading and collecting books in the digital and AI era, where traditional publishing and physical books are perceived to be in decline. Speaker 2 argues that while the medium of books has evolved over centuries, from stone carvings to digital texts, their essential function as a structured carrier of knowledge and culture remains unchanged.

# Key Takeaways
*   Reading and book collecting are becoming increasingly niche pursuits in the modern era.
*   The concept of a "book" as a cultural and knowledge carrier has existed for millennia, with its physical form evolving (e.g., from oracle bones to printed codexes to digital texts).
*   Despite changes in the medium, the fundamental essence of a book as a structured information and knowledge carrier persists.
*   The discussion is framed against a backdrop of skepticism about the relevance of books and traditional publishing in the age of AI.

*   **Speaker 2:** "书作为传统就是数字化之前人类千百年的文化的一个载体了...书的本质，我觉得到目前为止，包括电子文本的书，它还没变，它首先可以说它是一个结构化的信息的载体，更精确的说它是一个知识载体。"
